# Superman

## About the Movie
**Superman** is one of my favorite superhero movies. It tells the story of Clark Kent, a hero from the planet Krypton who protects Earth and inspires people through courage, hope, and selflessness.

### Main Characters
- Superman / Clark Kent
- Lois Lane
- Lex Luthor
- Jonathan Kent
- Martha Kent

### Superman's Powers
- Flight
- Super Strength
- Heat Vision
- X-Ray Vision
- Super Speed
